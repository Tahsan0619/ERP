# ERP Product Source

## Product name
BusinessOS ERP

## One-line summary
A modular web ERP for mid-market trading, distribution, and light manufacturing companies that need one system for sales, purchasing, inventory, finance, HR, and reporting.

## Problem
Growing businesses run operations across spreadsheets, accounting software, WhatsApp, and separate inventory tools. Data conflicts, stock mismatches, delayed invoices, weak audit trails, and slow month-end close.

## Target customers
1. Trading and wholesale distributors
2. Multi-warehouse retail or distribution businesses
3. Light manufacturing or assembly companies
4. Service-plus-product businesses with inventory

## Primary users
1. Owners and directors
2. Sales and account managers
3. Purchase and procurement staff
4. Warehouse and store keepers
5. Accountants and finance controllers
6. HR and payroll officers
7. System administrators

## Core modules
1. Identity and access
2. Organization and masters
3. CRM and sales
4. Purchasing and vendors
5. Inventory and warehouses
6. Manufacturing light (BOM and production orders)
7. Finance and accounting
8. HR and payroll
9. Reporting and dashboards
10. System settings and audit

## Product principles
1. One source of truth for stock, money, and people
2. Document-driven flows with approval where needed
3. Role-based access and full audit history
4. Modular rollout: start with MVP, enable modules later
5. Local tax and multi-currency ready design
6. Fast operational screens over heavy enterprise clutter

## MVP focus
Sales, purchasing, inventory, basic finance, users/roles, and core reports.

## Later phases
Manufacturing light, advanced HR/payroll, multi-branch consolidation, portal access, deep banking integrations, and advanced analytics.

## Constraints
1. Web-first responsive app
2. Multi-tenant capable architecture preferred
3. Strong audit and permission model required from day one
4. Must support multi-warehouse and multi-branch data separation
5. No em dash characters in product documentation files

## Open decisions
1. Exact tax regime defaults by country
2. Preferred stack (see tech.md)
3. Whether manufacturing is MVP or Phase 2
4. Whether employee self-service is included early
